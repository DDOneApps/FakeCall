FakeCall schedules simulated incoming calls using Android's Telecom framework, so the call appears in the device's regular phone experience instead of a custom fake-call screen.

<b>Features:</b>

<ul>
<li><b>Native phone UI:</b> incoming fake calls are routed through Android Telecom.</li>
<li><b>Scheduling:</b> start a call after a delay or from saved quick-trigger presets.</li>
<li><b>Custom caller details:</b> choose the displayed caller name, number, photo, ringtone behavior, and SIM/provider label.</li>
<li><b>Answer audio:</b> keep the call silent, play one selected audio file, use a folder-based MP3 IVR, or configure custom keypad audio flows.</li>
<li><b>Alarm mode:</b> create alarms that use fake-call behavior and optional spoken messages.</li>
<li><b>Call history:</b> optional call-log integration makes simulated calls easier to review later.</li>
<li><b>Recording:</b> optionally record microphone audio during a fake call.</li>
<li><b>Automation:</b> trigger calls from compatible automation apps or ADB via broadcast intents.</li>
<li><b>Shortcuts:</b> use app shortcuts, Quick Settings tiles, and the accessibility shortcut for faster triggering.</li>
</ul>

The app is free software and is designed to work without accounts, ads, or analytics.
